FAQ

Everything you need to know

Shopping

How do I know which fragrance is right for me?

We recommend starting with our Discovery Sets, which include 5 miniature versions of our most loved fragrances. You can also explore our fragrance notes guide or contact our team for a personalized recommendation.

Are your products authentic?

Absolutely. Every fragrance is sourced directly from the brand or authorized distributors. Each bottle is sealed, batch-coded, and comes with a certificate of authenticity.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes — every order ships in our signature black and white packaging with a complimentary ribbon. If you'd like a handwritten note or custom gift box, you can select this option at checkout.

Shipping

How long will my order take to arrive?

Orders are prepared within 1–2 business days. Standard delivery takes 3–7 days depending on your location. Express options are available at checkout for faster delivery.

Is shipping really free worldwide?

Yes — we offer complimentary tracked shipping to over 100 countries, with no minimum purchase required. Because luxury should never come with hidden costs.

Returns

What is your return policy?

You can return any unopened fragrance within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Return shipping is always free. Please note that opened or used fragrances cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.

How long does a refund take?

Once we receive your return, refunds are processed within 5–7 business days and issued to your original payment method. You'll receive an email confirmation the moment it's complete.
