How to Return

Three simple steps

01

Request a Return

Email us at returns@chanel-insp.com within 30 days of delivery with your order number. We'll send you a prepaid return label within 24 hours.

02

Package It

Place the unopened fragrance in its original packaging. Attach the prepaid label to the box and drop it at any authorized courier location near you.

03

Get Refunded

Once we receive your return, your refund will be processed within 5–7 business days. You'll receive a confirmation email the moment it's complete.

Return Policy

Everything you need to know

30 Days

Free Return Shipping

We cover all return shipping costs — worldwide, no exceptions.

Full Refund

Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–7 days.

Please Note

For hygiene and safety reasons, opened or used fragrances cannot be returned. Gift sets must be returned complete — all items included. Limited edition items are final sale.
