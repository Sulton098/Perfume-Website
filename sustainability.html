Our Commitment

At Chanel, sustainability is not a trend — it is a responsibility. We are dedicated to preserving the planet's resources, supporting the communities that cultivate our ingredients, and creating fragrances that are as kind to the earth as they are beautiful.

Our Pillars

Three areas of focus

Natural Ingredients

We source our ingredients from sustainable farms and cooperatives, ensuring that every harvest respects biodiversity and supports local communities.

Responsible Packaging

Our packaging is designed to be refillable, recyclable, and made from responsibly sourced materials. We are committed to reducing waste at every stage.

Community Support

We invest in the communities that grow our ingredients, providing fair wages, education, and resources to build a more equitable future for all.

Our Goals

Looking ahead

2026

Carbon Neutral Operations

Achieve carbon neutrality across all our production facilities and boutiques worldwide.

2028

Source all raw materials from certified sustainable suppliers with full traceability from farm to bottle.
